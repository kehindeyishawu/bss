Who We Are

Basic Sharp Services Nigeria Limited is a leading authorized distributor of Sharp products and a comprehensive solutions provider for businesses and homes across Nigeria. Incorporated under RC 445324, we operate with full regulatory compliance and a commitment to excellence at every touchpoint.

Our business spans four core categories — Office Equipment, Domestic Appliances, Consumables, and Support Services — giving our clients access to a complete Sharp ecosystem under one trusted roof.

We serve a diverse client base: corporate organizations, government institutions, educational establishments, SMEs, and private homes. Whatever your Sharp product need, our team has the expertise and inventory to deliver.

Under the leadership of our Managing Director & CEO, Olufemi L. Adebayo, BSS has built a reputation for reliability, technical competence, and customer-first service that keeps our clients coming back.
