Product Overview

Reliable Output
for the BP-30M
Series.

The Sharp BP-FT301 is the genuine OEM standard-yield black toner cartridge designed for the Sharp BP-30M series of A3 monochrome multifunction printers. Rated at approximately 8,600 pages at 5% coverage, it delivers consistent, high-quality monochrome output that meets the demands of a busy A3 workgroup.

As a genuine Sharp consumable, the BP-FT301 is engineered specifically for the BP-30M series print engine — ensuring optimal toner density, reliable feeding, and consistent output quality from the first page to the last. Using genuine toner also protects your machine's manufacturer warranty and ensures the best possible image quality.

The BP-FT301 is compatible with three models in the BP-30M series: the BP-30M28, BP-30M31, and BP-30M35. For offices with high monthly print volumes, we recommend reviewing your average monthly usage against the 8,600-page yield to determine the right replacement frequency for your operation.
