Product Overview

Consistent Output.
Every Page.

The Sharp BP-FT201 is the genuine OEM standard-yield black toner cartridge designed specifically for the Sharp BP-20M series of monochrome multifunction printers. With a rated yield of 10,000 pages at 5% coverage, it delivers sharp, consistent monochrome output across every page of its lifespan.

As a genuine Sharp consumable, the BP-FT201 is engineered to work in perfect harmony with the BP-20M series print engine — ensuring optimal print quality, reliable performance, and no compromise to your machine's warranty. It is the right choice for offices with moderate monthly print volumes that value quality and dependability over bulk.

For higher-volume environments, Sharp also offers the BP-GT200 high-yield variant, which extends yield to approximately 19,000–20,000 pages — reducing cost-per-page and replacement frequency significantly.
