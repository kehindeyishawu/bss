Product Overview

The Sharp BP-GT200 is the genuine OEM high-yield black toner cartridge for the BP-20M series monochrome MFPs. With an impressive yield of approximately 19,000 pages at 5% coverage, it is the ideal choice for busy offices and high-volume print environments where frequent cartridge changes are disruptive and costly.

Compared to the standard-yield BP-FT201 (10,000 pages), the BP-GT200 delivers nearly double the output per cartridge — significantly reducing cost-per-page and the frequency of toner replacements. As a genuine Sharp consumable, it is engineered to match the BP-20M series print engine precisely, ensuring consistent print quality, reliable feeding, and no impact on machine warranty.

The BP-GT200 is the recommended toner for organisations with monthly print volumes that regularly exceed several thousand pages. It keeps operations running smoothly, reduces consumables management overhead, and delivers the same crisp, sharp monochrome output as the standard-yield variant — just for significantly longer.
