Product Overview

The Fastest
in the Series.
Built to Perform.

The Sharp BP-30M35 is the top-tier model in the BP-30M series, delivering the fastest output at 35 pages per minute with a class-leading first copy time of just 4.0 seconds. It shares the same advanced platform as the BP-30M31 — 7-inch colour touchscreen, 4 GB memory, 128 GB SSD — but with a higher-speed print engine for the most demanding workgroups.

Scanning throughput reaches 60 originals per minute with the 100-sheet RSPF, and the extensive file format support — including searchable PDF, OOXML, and PDF/A — ensures every scanned document is ready for immediate use in any workflow. The BP-30M35 is the right choice when speed is the priority and downtime is not an option.

Optional Adobe PostScript 3 and optional fax capability (MX-FX15) make this a fully-featured enterprise-class device. Optional 512 GB SSD upgrade (BP-SD10) extends document filing capacity to 20,000 pages across 3,000 files for high-volume archiving environments.

What It Does

Key Features

🚀
35 ppm — Top Speed in Series

At 35 pages per minute with a 4.0-second first copy time, the BP-30M35 is Sharp's fastest model in the BP-30M lineup — designed for high-volume workgroups where every second counts.

🖥️
7-Inch Colour Touchscreen

The large, intuitive colour LCD touchscreen provides a modern, app-like user experience — reducing operator training time and making complex multi-step jobs simple and fast.

💾
4 GB Memory + 128 GB SSD

Substantial onboard memory and SSD storage enable high-volume document filing, fast multi-page job processing, and optional expansion to 512 GB for enterprise-scale archiving.

📄
1,200 × 600 dpi Print Resolution

Sharp's enhanced print engine delivers crisp 1,200 × 600 dpi output — producing fine text, intricate line art, and detailed graphics with exceptional clarity.

📁
Advanced Document Filing

Standard capacity of 5,000 pages across main, custom, and quick file folders, expandable to 20,000 pages / 3,000 files with the optional BP-SD10 SSD upgrade.

📠
Optional Fax & PostScript 3

Add the MX-FX15 fax module for full Super G3/G3 fax capability, or the MX-PK13 kit for Adobe PostScript 3 — making the BP-30M35 a truly complete office document system.
